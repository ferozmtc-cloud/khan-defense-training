Why This Is Different

Self-Defense
That Actually Holds Up.

Most self-defense courses teach choreography — moves that work against a cooperative training partner in a quiet room. Real violence is fast, close, and chaotic. It doesn't wait for you to remember step three.

Coach Khan trains you the way top-tier tactical and combat programs actually operate: address the threat before it happens, control distance and position, and have answers that work under real adrenaline — not just on a mat.

Training Framework Live Curriculum
👁️
Awareness & Prevention
Recognizing pre-incident cues and environmental risk before a situation becomes physical. The best fight is the one you saw coming and avoided.
🗣️
De-Escalation & Distance Control
Verbal de-escalation, positioning, and creating space — the skills that prevent 90% of confrontations from ever reaching contact.
🥊
Striking & Clinch Control
Functional striking, hand-fighting, and clinch work drawn directly from competition — built for real bodies in real chaos, not choreography.
🛡️
Ground Defense & Escapes
What to do when the fight goes to the ground — getting back to your feet safely, and defending against a bigger, heavier attacker.
🧠
Stress Inoculation
Pressure-tested drilling so your body knows what to do when adrenaline floods your system and fine motor skills disappear.
